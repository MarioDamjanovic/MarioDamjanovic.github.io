My mom is head of a cybersecurity team at a hospital and tells me plenty of stories of some crazy tickets she has gotten, and I find it super interesting. There are loads of people that know a lot less about technology and have a lot less common sense when it comes to technology as well. I know sometimes it can be stressful, but in all, it just seems like a field I would enjoy; I love solving problems and this field would give me plenty of problems all the time to solve.
